# PRODUCT — Luma Okey

## Pitch

A calm, luminous Okey room for trustworthy Classic and 101 matches with friends or deterministic offline bots.

## Target user & trigger moment

- **Who:** Turkish- and English-speaking adults (18+) who want a polished evening tabletop session on phone or tablet.
- **Trigger:** “We want to play now, but the current apps feel noisy, unreliable, or cannot put our friends into the same room.”
- **Alternatives & wedge:** incumbent Okey networks and simple offline apps. Luma Okey combines reliable invite rooms, replayable deterministic fairness, offline continuity, device-personal ambience, and a calm premium table; monetization never changes gameplay.

## Opportunity thesis

- **Evidence:** six public Turkish App Store competitors and recent one-star review themes inspected on 2026-08-11; see `docs/research-2026-08-11.md`.
- **Evidence strength:** moderate; product/listing/review observations are sourced, market size/revenue/CAC are unknown.
- **Positioning:** For adults who play Okey to relax and connect, Luma Okey is the social tabletop game that makes every turn clear and every room dependable because the rules are deterministic, online state is authoritative, and economy is separated from fairness.
- **Primary acquisition:** Turkish short-form tabletop/game creators and direct friend groups; the luminous deal-and-discard sequence is understandable without sound.
- **Gotcha moment:** a soft-lit table deals readable tiles into a tactile rack, one drag/discard passes the turn halo, and the whole state is understood in five silent seconds.
- **Three-second demo:** drag, reorder, discard, turn handoff—no modal and no casino clutter.
- **Video-ready state:** seeded offline Classic round with four avatars, readable rack, turn halo, discard arc, and reduced UI chrome.
- **Opportunity score:** 80/100 — `go`.
- **Critical risks:** crowded category; perceived unfairness; social abuse; realtime/voice reliability; paid-chip stake classification. The clone screen passes only if fairness/replay and calm reliable rooms are visible in the shipped core loop. Purchased-chip stake rooms remain production-off.

## The ONE core feature

> A responsive, tactile four-seat table where Classic or 101 rules remain legible, deterministic, resumable, and fair online or offline.

**Exceptionally-well bar:**

- A first-time player identifies their rack, turn, legal next action, picked tile, and discard target without instruction.
- The same seed and command sequence yields the same result; online reconnect and duplicate commands cannot fork the room.
- Tile interaction holds 60 FPS on target devices with a controlled 30 FPS fallback and an equivalent Reduced Motion path.

## Vision and boundaries

- **Long term:** the most trusted calm digital Okey table for close friends, with a portable browser client in V2.
- **MVP bet:** users return because the table feels excellent and rooms work, not because of artificial scarcity.
- **Will not become:** cash gambling, prize competition, chip transfer marketplace, manipulative random-item casino, public voice-discovery network, or pay-to-win game.

## Monetization

- **Model:** hybrid consumable chips + VIP, behind mock-first adapters.
- **Entitlement/offering:** `vip`; `default` and `onb_discount` reserved for RevenueCat setup.
- **Products:** three chip packages plus weekly/yearly VIP; IDs/prices are human TODOs.
- **Free tier:** 5,000 starting chips, free casual rooms, daily streak 250/300/350/400/500/750/1,000.
- **VIP value:** cosmetics, ad-free experience, and bonus convenience only; no rule, deal, matchmaking, or scoring advantage.
- **Hard production gate:** purchased-chip stake rooms disabled until store classification and legal review are approved.

## Launch thesis and success

- **Marginal cost:** unknown until realtime/voice usage tests; casual offline play is near-zero provider cost.
- **Gross-margin target:** ≥70% after stores, voice, and infrastructure.
- **Break-even CAC:** unknown until D7 retention and paid conversion exist.
- **90-day thesis:** recruit small friend groups through honest short-form demos, make room invites reliable, and let organic retention prove whether VIP deserves scale.
- **First hook:** “Okey masası sonunda sakin: bir davet, dört kişi, tek bakışta sıra kimde.”
- **Go/kill:** keep building while seeded/offline determinism and reconnect acceptance pass; after launch target ≥15% D7 room return and ≥60% invite-to-seated conversion. Reposition after two measured iterations if trust/reliability remain the primary complaint.

| Metric | First-90-day target |
|---|---:|
| First session → completed round | ≥65% |
| Invite link → seated room | ≥60% |
| D1 retention | ≥30% |
| D7 room return | ≥15% |
| Crash-free sessions | ≥99.5% |
| Voice-enabled room with successful reconnect | ≥95% |
| Paid conversion | observe before setting a target |

- **Screenshot 1:** “One calm table. Every turn is clear.”
