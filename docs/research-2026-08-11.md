# Public product research — 2026-08-11

## Method and limits

Public Turkish App Store listing metadata and the latest public one-star review feed were inspected on 2026-08-11. Ratings/counts are point-in-time observations, not market size, revenue, or causality. Review themes below are paraphrased; review IDs retain traceability without copying review text. Downloads, revenue, paid acquisition, and organic-growth claims remain unknown.

Relevant substitute behavior also comes from polished digital tabletop games and Discord-style voice rooms: users expect reconnect, friend invites, mute/block/report, and legible state at a glance. These are design expectations, not evidence of a particular competitor implementation.

## Repeated signals

1. **Observed:** ad interruption and economy distrust recur across multiple direct competitors.
2. **Observed:** users interpret unexplained randomness or loss streaks as manipulation.
3. **Observed:** friend-room routing and account continuity failures are high-friction social failures.
4. **Sourced from listings:** online rooms, voice/chat, offline bots, leagues, and virtual economies are already common; feature presence alone is not differentiation.
5. **Assumption to validate:** a calmer premium-feeling table, transparent replayable fairness, and device-personal ambience can win a narrow group of adults who want a relaxed evening room rather than a casino-like feed.

## Original wedge and clone screen

The category is crowded, so “better designed Okey” alone fails the clone screen. Luma Okey passes only with a screen-visible wedge: **a calm, luminous table that makes every tile action understandable and replayable, lets friends enter a reliable room in one invite, and keeps monetization outside game fairness.** The seeded offline engine and authoritative sequenced online protocol make that promise testable.

## Name screen

App Store queries for `Luma Okey`, `Niva Okey`, and `Masa Nova` were inspected on 2026-08-11. No exact `Luma Okey` or `Niva Okey` listing appeared; `Masa Nova` is more semantically generic. `Luma Okey` is recommended for its relationship to the luminous design system. This is not a trademark opinion; formal clearance remains a human TODO before store registration.
