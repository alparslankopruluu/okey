# Growth Plan — Luma Okey

## Thesis

- **North star:** completed friend-room rounds per weekly active group.
- **Primary channel:** Turkish short-form creators and direct friend-group invites.
- **Why:** the silent five-second table interaction is visual, while one invite naturally recruits the other three seats.
- **Referral loop:** room invite → four seated players → rematch; no rewarded/incentivized reviews.
- **CAC/LTV:** unknown until D7 retention and paid conversion exist. No paid scale before one organic format repeats.
- **Current MRR rung:** rung 1 ($0 → first $100); prove that 20 adults value reliable rooms/VIP convenience.

## Funnel baseline

| Stage | Baseline | First target | Window |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Store page → download | unknown | observe | first 30 days |
| Download → completed offline round | unknown | 65% | first 500 installs |
| Invite → seated friend room | unknown | 60% | first 100 invites |
| D1 / D7 return | unknown | 30% / 15% | first 500 activated users |
| Store view → purchase | unknown | observe only | after sandbox/live catalog |

## Store and creative

- **Screenshot hooks:** calm/clear table; Classic + 101 online/offline; reliable friend invite; personal music and safe voice.
- **First two:** “One calm table. Every turn is clear.” / “Classic or 101—with friends or bots.”
- **Demo:** one drag/reorder/discard/turn-handoff clip from a real seeded build.
- **PPO:** after release, vary only screenshot-one composition; product-page conversion; ≥7 days and adequate sample; stop on material regression.
- **CPP:** friend-group intent and “101 with friends” keyword/creative may deep-link to room join only after the real route ships.
- **In-App Event:** launch-night friend-room session is only a draft opportunity.
- **Review moment:** after a completed rematch with no recent crash/report/purchase issue; never during onboarding or for a reward.
